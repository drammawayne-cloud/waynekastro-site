PAPERLOV Android app privacy

Effective October 27, 2026. This policy applies to PAPERLOV Android versions 1.0 and 1.1, operated by Rich Row Records LLC. It does not describe the separate website or connected-bank services.

Local information

The app processes notebook descriptions, dates and money-in/money-out amounts on your device. Saved information stays in private app storage. The Android app has no account, bank connection or payments. Version 1.0 has no advertisements, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking or network permission. Version 1.1 adds internet access for the advertising and privacy features described below. The app does not transmit your saved records or inputs to us.

Your choices and deletion

You choose whether to share a scenario or export a file using Android system controls. Review the information, destination and recipients first. Services you choose handle the information you send under their own policies. Use Delete record or Erase this notebook in Guide & privacy to remove records. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Automatic Android app backup is disabled. Exported or shared copies are separate and must be deleted where you saved or sent them.

Support and external links

If you contact support, we receive information you voluntarily send and use it to respond. External privacy and support pages open in your browser. Their hosting provider processes ordinary technical network request information to deliver and protect the pages. Do not send passwords, account numbers, banking credentials or sensitive financial documents.

Contact

Rich Row Records LLC · hello@paperlov.com. Contact us with privacy questions or requests to delete support correspondence.

Android app support

Advertising in Android version 1.1 and later

Updated November 14, 2026. PAPERLOV Android version 1.1 introduces an optional-to-view banner in Guide & privacy. Store availability depends on review and rollout; this policy does not mean ads are approved or serving. Version 1.0 remains ad-free. This section does not enable native ads in any iOS app.

Version 1.1 and later use Google Mobile Ads to help fund the app. Google may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The SDK encrypts this information in transit. Non-personalized advertising still uses data for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

The app checks regional consent requirements before initializing advertising. Open Guide & privacy → Ad privacy choices to review or withdraw consent where required, or to retry a failed privacy check. Ads stay hidden when a consent check fails. The app requests non-personalized ads; limited ads may still be available after declining consent, where Google permits them. Declining consent, a failed consent check or an unavailable ad does not block the app's main features. Consult the version's release notes for availability. We do not provide calculator inputs, financial notebook records, account passwords or private member content to Google as ad targeting data. Advertising does not require precise-location, camera or microphone access.

Google's own retention and user controls are described in Google's Privacy Policy and information for users of partner apps. Device advertising settings can also let you reset or delete an advertising identifier. The existing contact and deletion routes on this page continue to apply to information held by the app operator. Installing an update does not make locally stored financial records public.
