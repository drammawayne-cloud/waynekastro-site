AssetsXray Android app privacy

Effective October 27, 2026. This policy applies to AssetsXray Android version 1.0, operated by Rich Row Records LLC. It does not describe the separate website or connected-bank services.

Local information

The app processes savings-plan inputs and calculator scenarios on your device. Saved information stays in private app storage. The Android app has no account, bank connection, payments, advertisements, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking or network permission. The app does not transmit your saved records or inputs to us.

Your choices and deletion

You choose whether to share a scenario or export a file using Android system controls. Review the information, destination and recipients first. Services you choose handle the information you send under their own policies. Reset saved savings plan in Help & privacy restores the example values. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Automatic Android app backup is disabled. Exported or shared copies are separate and must be deleted where you saved or sent them.

Support and external links

If you contact support, we receive information you voluntarily send and use it to respond. External privacy and support pages open in your browser. Their hosting provider processes ordinary technical network request information to deliver and protect the pages. Do not send passwords, account numbers, banking credentials or sensitive financial documents.
