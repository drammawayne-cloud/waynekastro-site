Wayne Kastro app support

Help for the Wayne Kastro iPhone, iPad and Android apps.

Contact app support

Email richrowrecords@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, operating-system version and a description of the problem. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or purchase tokens.

Sign-in and member access

Open Account in the app and sign in with the account used for your membership. For a purchase that is not showing, use Restore Purchases on iPhone or iPad, or Restore Google Play purchases on Android. Contact app support if access is still missing.

Public releases and radio can be explored without a membership. Member content depends on the subscription and selections associated with your account.

Manage or cancel subscriptions

Subscriptions are managed by the store where you purchased them. Manage Apple subscriptions or manage Google Play subscriptions. Memberships and Teach Me Simply companions are separate subscriptions.

Privacy and account deletion

Read the app privacy policy for data use and retention details. In the app, open Account, sign in and choose Delete my account. You may also email richrowrecords@gmail.com from your registered address to request account deletion. We may verify ownership. Requests are handled within 30 days, except records that must be retained for legal, payment or security obligations.

The customer account is shared across Rich Row artist services. Account deletion affects that shared account and does not cancel Apple or Google Play subscriptions; cancel each subscription separately through its store.
