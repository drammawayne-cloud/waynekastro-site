PAPERLOV Android app support

Record a description and a positive USD amount, choose whether money is coming in, then tap Save record. Your totals and records stay on this device. Export CSV spreadsheet opens Android’s file picker so you can choose where to save the file.

To remove records, choose Delete record and confirm, or Guide → Erase this notebook. Exported copies remain separate. The notebook supports up to 5,000 records.
