Artist accounts, broadcasts and support payments

The private portal stores account and artist information, release metadata, uploaded material and label messages for the requested services. Do not post private information in public chat.

Wayne Kastro iPhone and iPad app

Updated October 11, 2026. The native app uses your email address and account identifier for sign-in. If you use a supported social sign-in option, its verified identity and any name supplied by that provider are retained with your account. Sign in with Apple can use Apple’s private email relay. The app stores its session in the device Keychain. Apple handles subscription payment details; we store verified transaction identifiers, product, renewal/expiry and refund information to provide the access you purchased. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

We store your selected artist books, monthly songwriting briefs and our responses to deliver those services. Teach Me Simply is an additional Apple subscription for the companions to your selected books. Its lessons are read within the app, with no download or sharing controls. Screen-recording detection hides lessons; no technical measure guarantees that a person cannot capture or photograph content.

Your saved-release favorites stay on your device. Our service providers process account, content delivery and necessary security information. Cloudflare Turnstile provides the secure check for email sign-in and may process IP addresses and browser or device signals to prevent automated abuse. Supabase provides account authentication and stores the account and access records. The native app does not contain an advertising SDK or use cross-app advertising tracking. Opening an official release or other external link subjects that site to its own privacy practices. Artist broadcasting through the owner console has its separate camera and microphone permissions.

Initiate full customer-account deletion from Account in the app. Requests are handled within 30 days, except records required for legal, payment or security obligations. The Rich Row customer account is shared across artist services, so deletion affects that shared account. Deleting it does not cancel an Apple subscription: cancel each subscription separately in Apple settings. Contact richrowfinancial@gmail.com for app privacy or deletion questions.
