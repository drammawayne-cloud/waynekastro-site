Wayne Kastro

Wayne Kastro brings the music and creative vision of Rich Row Records into focus. His catalog includes Trapped Inside, Money Making Machine and a growing collection of recordings released under the Wayne Kastro name. From individual singles to collaborations, his artist profile brings those releases together in one place, with direct access to the music and its official visuals.

Biography

Also known professionally as Dramma Wayne, his work connects performance with production. Aroma pairs Wayne Kastro with Shorts Temper, while his wider catalog documents years of releases and creative partnerships. The credits attached to each recording remain part of that story, giving listeners a clear connection between the artist, the collaborators and the music they helped create.
